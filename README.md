# 🌌 THE_SPACE

**Bringing Forgotten Space Explorers Back to Life**

## 📖 About the Project

THE_SPACE is an interactive, story-driven educational web experience designed to introduce school students and casual space enthusiasts to the fascinating hardware and robotic explorers left behind on the Moon, Mars, and beyond.

The project aims to transform space history into an engaging storytelling experience by exploring the machines, instruments, and equipment sent on space missions. Rather than simply presenting facts, THE_SPACE seeks to tell the stories behind these technological achievements — why they were built, what missions they served, and the scientific discoveries they helped humanity make.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Make space exploration history engaging and accessible.
- Introduce audiences to the hardware and robotic explorers sent on space missions.
- Explain the purpose, functionality, and significance of different space artifacts.
- Present real mission histories and scientific discoveries through storytelling.
- Encourage curiosity about space science, engineering, and exploration.

## 🌍 Vision

Our vision is to make the history of space exploration more than just a collection of dates and facts. We want audiences to discover the stories of the machines that travelled beyond Earth, the challenges they faced, and the knowledge they left behind.

Even when a mission ends, its contribution to science continues.

**Every artifact has a story. Let's discover it.**
